## 1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

1.1 "Confidential Information" means any and all information, in any form or medium, disclosed by Medina Tech to Recipient, including but not limited to:

(a) **Technical Information:** Source code, algorithms, mathematical formulations, system architectures, data structures, protocols, specifications, designs, inventions, processes, techniques, and know-how;

(b) **Business Information:** Business plans, strategies, customer lists, pricing information, marketing plans, financial information, and operational data;

(c) **Intellectual Property:** Patents (pending and issued), patent applications, trade secrets, copyrights, trademarks, and any other proprietary rights;

(d) **Research and Development:** Research findings, experimental results, prototypes, development plans, and technical roadmaps;

## 2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECIPIENT

2.1 **Non-Disclosure:** Recipient shall not disclose any Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of Medina Tech.

2.2 **Limited Use:** Recipient shall use Confidential Information solely for the Permitted Purpose and for no other purpose.

2.3 **Standard of Care:** Recipient shall protect Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.

2.4 **Access Restriction:** Recipient shall limit access to Confidential Information to those of its employees, contractors, and agents who:
(a) Have a need to know for the Permitted Purpose;
(b) Have been informed of the confidential nature of the information; and
(c) Are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement.

2.5 **No Reverse Engineering:** Recipient shall not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, or otherwise attempt to derive the source code, structure, or design of any Confidential Information.

2.6 **No Copying:** Recipient shall not copy or reproduce any Confidential Information except as necessary for the Permitted Purpose and with appropriate confidentiality markings.

## 3. EXCLUSIONS

3.1 Confidential Information does not include information that:

(a) Was publicly known at the time of disclosure through no fault of Recipient;

(b) Becomes publicly known after disclosure through no fault of Recipient;

(c) Was rightfully in Recipient's possession before disclosure, as evidenced by written records;

(d) Is rightfully obtained by Recipient from a third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation; or

(e) Is independently developed by Recipient without use of or reference to Confidential Information, as evidenced by written records.

3.2 A disclosure required by law, regulation, or court order shall not be considered a breach, provided Recipient:
(a) Gives Medina Tech prompt written notice of such requirement;
(b) Cooperates with Medina Tech's efforts to obtain protective treatment; and
(c) Discloses only the minimum information required.
