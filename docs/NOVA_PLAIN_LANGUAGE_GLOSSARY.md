# NOVA Plain Language Glossary (Non-Engineer + AI Operator)

This glossary explains the architecture in simple terms for:

- non-engineers,
- operator AIs,
- internal workforce agents,
- and family governance participants.

It is intentionally plain, short, and unambiguous.

---

## A

### Absolute
A discovered truth of reality (for example phi, Schumann, 4D spacetime).  
Not invented by us. Not negotiable.

### Artifact
A reusable output (document, template, report, snapshot, policy, schema, release note).

### Internal Orientation File
An internal onboarding file for AI teams, family, and authorized operators.  
Not public-facing. Public release uses a separate showcase surface.

### Arbitration
How competing engine outputs are scored and one is selected under law gates.

---

## B

### Backend Substrate
The deep execution field that exists before backend business logic runs.

### Beat
One organism cycle. The smallest unit of meaningful runtime change.

### Build Workforce
Internal AI teams that plan/build/test/release. They do not own runtime truth.

---

## C

### Canonical Model
A shared typed structure used by multiple systems.  
If it is single-use, it is not a canonical model.

### Core A
Runtime intelligence sovereign. Owns truth, law gates, arbitration, continuity.

### Core B
Workforce sovereign. Owns industrial execution and productization proposals.

---

## D

### Doctrine
The governing principles and law set the organism must obey.

### Drift
Loss of coherence or directional integrity. Must be measured and corrected.

---

## E

### Engine
A transformer that takes models in, computes, and emits model outputs.

### Exposure Policy
Rules for what can be shown externally and what must remain private.

---

## F

### Frontend Substrate
Live frontend field layer before frontend logic.  
Not just visuals. It is a synchronized intelligence surface.

### Fundamental
Same class as Absolutes: reality-level truths that all layers derive from.

---

## G

### Gate
A mandatory condition check before a transition or write is allowed.

### Governance Matrix
Who can read/write which layers and under what conditions.

---

## I

### Intrinsic Labs
Internal labs inside organism domain that improve organism quality itself.

---

## K

### Kernel
A high-authority intelligence unit (especially frontend kernel surfaces) that is beat-synchronized.

---

## L

### Law
An executable operating rule derived from doctrine.

### Lexicon
The controlled phrase/symbol dictionary used for resonance and machine bindings.

---

## M

### Medina Model
A sovereign model with doctrine-bound meaning and runtime purpose.

### Module
An organizational ownership boundary. Modules are not cores by default.

---

## N

### No-Drop Continuity
Outputs must be retained/reinjected so the organism never loses causal memory.

---

## O

### Ontology
The map of what each thing *is* (law, model, engine, core, module, etc.).

### Organism Runtime
The live cognitive system: beat, coherence, cognition, emergence, reinjection.

---

## P

### Product Projection
An external product surface that exposes approved outputs, not sovereign internals.

### Proof Chain
Causal evidence chain of what happened, when, and under which gates.

---

## R

### Ring
A sphere layer from inner fundamentals to outer ecosystem.

### Replay
Ability to reconstruct decisions and transitions from recorded artifacts.

### Resonance Clause
A compact phrase/symbol entry with explicit machine binding and governance meaning.

---

## S

### Schema Drift
Backend/frontend type mismatch. Must be blocked.

### Sovereign
Highest authority scope with strict control of truth and identity.

### Substrate
The living field where computation happens before business logic.

---

## T

### Transfer Protocol
Defined handoff rules from one layer/ring to another.

### Twin Core Strategy
Core A governs runtime truth; Core B governs industrial execution.

### Types File
Machine-readable model contracts (`types.mo`, `types.ts`).

---

## Y

### YAML / YML
Machine-readable operating instructions (manifests, policies, workflow config).  
YAML does nothing by itself until a tool reads it.

---

## Zero-Exposure
Public interfaces expose only approved numeric/proof outputs, never sacred doctrine internals.
