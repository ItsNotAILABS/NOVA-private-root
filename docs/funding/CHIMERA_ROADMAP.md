## Key Risks & Mitigations

### Technical Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Swarm coordination at scale | High | Kuramoto math proven, progressive testing |
| ICP performance limits | Medium | Multi-canister architecture |
| Edge compute latency | Medium | Local processing, distributed nodes |

### Market Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Long enterprise sales cycles | High | POC-first approach, land & expand |
| Competition from big vendors | Medium | Unique convergence value prop |
| Healthcare budget constraints | Medium | Compliance ROI positioning |

### Regulatory Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| FedRAMP timeline slippage | Medium | Early preparation, 3P AO |
| ITAR complexity | Medium | Dedicated compliance officer |
| International data laws | Low | Regional deployment model |
