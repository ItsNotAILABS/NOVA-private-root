\begin{abstract}
Healthcare cyber defense faces an existential crisis: 725 breaches exposing 133 million records in 2023, with average breach costs exceeding \$10.93M. Traditional security systems—rule-based firewalls, signature-matching intrusion detection, periodic compliance audits—fail because they are static while adversaries are adaptive. We introduce \CHIMERA{}, a \emph{living organism defense system} built on the \NOVA{} sovereign AGI platform, governed by ten immutable laws encoded in Motoko smart contracts on the Internet Computer (\ICP{}). \CHIMERA{} deploys 21 living organisms (4 products + 13 team entities + 4 compliance verifiers) that maintain continuous audit-readiness across 481 compliance controls (SOC 2: 64, FedRAMP: 325, HIPAA: 54, ITAR: 38). Key innovations include: (1) the \emph{No-Drop Law} ensuring skill floors of 0.01, (2) \emph{Hebbian compounding} for team coherence, (3) \emph{Kuramoto synchronization} ($R \geq \PHIINV$) for swarm defense, (4) \emph{Golden angle formation} (137.5°) for optimal drone coverage, and (5) \emph{Anti-Family classification} (6 threat levels) with automatic response escalation. We prove that \CHIMERA{}'s $\PHI$-weighted coupling achieves certification readiness with pass rate $\geq 0.95$ and zero critical failures. The system is positioned for the healthcare cyber defense market with tiered pricing (\$25K–\$2.5M MRR) targeting the 100 largest US hospital systems.
\end{abstract}

\begin{definition}[Living Organism Defense]

\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Growth}: Capability increases monotonically with experience
  \item \textbf{Adaptation}: Response profiles evolve based on threat landscape
  \item \textbf{Coherence}: Multiple subsystems synchronize for emergent behavior
  \item \textbf{Compounding}: Current skills bootstrap future learning
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\CHIMERA{} implements this hypothesis through 10 immutable laws encoded in Motoko smart contracts on the Internet Computer, providing formal guarantees unavailable in traditional security architectures.
