This paper series documents the NOVA (PARALLAX) organism — not as a technical manual,
but as a living research record. Each paper captures a discovery, a theory, or a journey.

**Paper A** is the personal paper — the story of building NOVA without knowing you were
building NOVA. The moment of realization. The journey through fractures.

the tool registry, the incentive fields. The infrastructure for the ecosystem.
